\# AI Tech Consultant — AI Usage Log



\## 1. Purpose



AI assistance was used during the development of this capstone project to support implementation, debugging, testing, and documentation.



\## 2. Areas Where AI Assisted



\### Task 1: Knowledge Brain

\- Assisted with Python code for collecting support article URLs and extracting article content.

\- Helped implement the FastAPI health-check and keyword-search endpoints.

\- Assisted with troubleshooting article ingestion and validating the saved dataset.

\- Helped prepare API documentation.



\### Task 2: Inbox Agent

\- Assisted with implementing email priority detection, categorization, and suggested reply generation.

\- Helped diagnose and correct a unit-test failure caused by keyword matching order.

\- Assisted with creating unit tests and documentation.



\### Task 3: Meeting to Scope

\- Assisted with implementing extraction of requirements, deliverables, and open questions from meeting notes.

\- Helped prepare unit tests and documentation.



\### Project Documentation

\- Assisted with drafting the README, system design, evaluation results, and this AI usage log.



\## 3. Human Review and Verification



The project developer ran the commands, reviewed the outputs, and verified the reported test results. The Knowledge Brain dataset was checked for missing titles, empty content, and duplicate URLs. The Inbox Agent and Meeting to Scope unit tests were executed to verify their current behavior.



AI-generated code and documentation should be reviewed and updated when requirements change. The recorded results describe the tests that were run; they do not guarantee production readiness.



\## 4. Limitations



\- The Inbox Agent currently processes supplied email data and does not connect to a real email account or send replies.

\- The Knowledge Brain uses keyword-based search and has not undergone a comprehensive semantic-relevance evaluation.

\- The Meeting to Scope component uses rule-based extraction and may not interpret every meeting note correctly.

\- Further testing, security review, and integration work may be needed before production use.
